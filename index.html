Where do you deliver? +

We currently deliver across Germany and selected EU destinations. Your delivery options and duties, if applicable, are shown at checkout.

Are the bottles and cans authentic? +

Yes. Every bottle and can in our collection is sourced through our verified import partners and arrives sealed and ready to share.

Can you help with a large event order? +

Absolutely. Tell us your date, location, and estimated bottle count. We will come back with a tailored quote and delivery plan.

Do I need to be 18 to order? +

Yes. You must be of legal drinking age in your country to browse, order, and receive alcohol from Liq Budz.
